# Phrase Pocket design

A pocket conversation journal inspired by the inspected Snow Pocket layout: fine dark outlines, offset paper shadows, illustrated scene companions and compact app navigation. The primary task is a short conversation quest, followed by a clear memory summary.

Use ice paper #F3F8FA, ink #243D46, muted #526A72, teal action #25636A, white cards and sunny #F5D67C accents. Yellow marks selected dates and collected stamps, never small body text. A small fox with speech cards anchors the identity; illustrations stay clear of inputs. Mobile uses three bottom destinations; desktop uses a compact sidebar. Use 44px minimum controls, safe-area spacing, visible focus and reduced-motion support.

All educational content and interface copy is English. Learning, recalling with help, independent recall and remembered across days are distinct. Due date is independent of memory state. Story completion never implies mastery.
